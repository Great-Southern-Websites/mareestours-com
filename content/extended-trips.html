About Bright and the Chalet

Bright is one of the most sought-after destinations for groups in Australia, positioned in North-East Victoria's sub alpine region. Bright is renowned Australia wide for its magnificent autumn trees, spectacular winters and beautiful spring gardens. Bright Chalet is a cosy alpine getaway in the heart of North East Victoria with scenic mountain views, offering a variety of charming accommodation. The Chalet offers the ideal blend of relaxation and recreation that makes a group tour a very special trip, and treats all guests as special with individual attention.

The Chalet was built in late 1988 in a European style. Rooms provide king size or twin accommodation, all with ensuite, remote control television with in-house video and satellite available. There are twenty-six rooms in all, well equipped with reverse cycle air-conditioning and opening onto a balcony. The Chalet is large enough to ensure guests are able to relax in spacious comfort. It has a delightful first-class restaurant and guests have a wide choice of food. Meals are provided in this package (except in transit). Breakfast consists of a buffet and cooked selection, a delicious buffet is served at night, and morning tea is supplied while on tour at the Chalet. There is a coffee lounge, a cocktail lounge, and a guest lounge with an open fire.

5 day itinerary

  1. Day 1

    Seaford to Bright

    Dinner included

    Depart Melbourne Monday 31 August at 10.00 am from your club at Seaford and travel to Bright, stopping for morning tea at Wallan. After a lunch stop (own cost) at Euroa, we travel to Bright passing Benalla and Glenrowan, then via the Ovens Valley Highway. While on route we pass through the towns of Myrtleford and Porepunkah, then on to the Bright Chalet where you will settle in for four nights of great food and enjoyment. The bar will open and dinner will be served on arrival.

  2. Day 2

    Harrietville and Wandiligong

    Breakfast, morning tea and dinner

    Bright is alive, with the backdrop of exotic trees ablaze in red and gold to provide a carpet of colours, and the spring harvest heralding the change of seasons. After breakfast we drive to Harrietville, visiting the bakery and the historic gold settlement. We then visit Wandiligong, a 10 minute drive from Bright. This picturesque valley is classified a heritage town by the National Trust. The Alpine Park provides a setting for a pleasant picnic, to discover historic buildings and gold diggings, and to visit the largest apple orchard in the southern hemisphere. Back to Bright for lunch (own cost) and a wander around the streets of Bright, maybe spending some time at the Bright Art Gallery. Then home for dinner.

  3. Day 3

    Mount Buffalo, Albury and Bonegilla

    Breakfast, morning tea and dinner

    After breakfast we depart the Chalet at 8.30 am for a visit to Mount Buffalo and the historic Mount Buffalo Chalet for a fantastic view of the Ovens Valley, where you will have morning tea. An option this morning is to visit Mount Hotham and Dinner Plain to see the snow and the Victorian ski fields. We then travel to Albury for lunch and visit the Bonegilla Migrant Centre before heading home for dinner.

  4. Day 4

    Myrtleford and Beechworth

    Breakfast, morning tea and dinner

    This morning we are off to the Pumpkin Seed Factory in Myrtleford. It is the largest producer of pumpkin seed products and has a great variety of tastings and sales. Then on to Beechworth, a beautiful and historic township, where we collect our tour guide for an hour's guided tour of Beechworth, its history and historical buildings. There will be time to check out the old shops, wander around and find out more about Ned Kelly. The Lace Shop is a great place to visit. The Beechworth Bakery is an award-winning attraction where everyone stops for lunch. The Rock Cavern has a great display of precious and semi-precious gemstones. Billson's Brewery is a must, with the largest collection of bottles in the southern hemisphere; beer and spirits are brewed here, and fine cordials are also made and sold. Purchase some honey from the award-winning Beechworth honey shop, with tastings available. From here we are off to the Red Stag Deer and Emu Farm, a most interesting place to visit, where the talk on the deer and the by-products of deer harvesting is very entertaining. Then it is time to return home and freshen up for dinner.

  5. Day 5

    Milawa and home

    Breakfast and morning tea

    We depart the Chalet at about 9.00 am after a hearty breakfast to return to Melbourne. Just after Myrtleford we turn onto the old snow road and travel via Milawa, where we stop at the Milawa Cheese Factory around 11 am (entry included) for a chance to purchase a variety of cheeses, all made on site. We stop at the Milawa Bakery for morning tea. We then travel to the Hume Highway, stopping for lunch at the roadhouse at Euroa, then on to Melbourne, arriving at Seaford at around 4.00 pm.
