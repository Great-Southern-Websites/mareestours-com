A big thank you to all those who promote Maree's Tours to your clubs and friends. It is always a shame when a trip is cancelled due to lack of numbers. If you belong to a club and have had a good trip, please let me know so I can investigate it further. New ideas are always welcomed.

Remember to cancel a booking if you cannot come. Please pay prior to the trip so I can confirm numbers. If for some reason you have not paid, please let me know so that I can confirm your booking.

I look forward to making many more happy memories with you all.

Maree HutchinsMaree's Tours
